Third-party data in this directory (excluded from the project's MIT license)

places.json.gz
  Airports and railway stations extracted from OpenStreetMap by
  tools/osm/extract_places.py.
  © OpenStreetMap contributors. Available under the Open Database License
  (ODbL) 1.0: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/
  This derived database is likewise licensed under the ODbL.

railway_timetable.json.gz
  Source: Fintraffic / digitraffic.fi, license CC 4.0 BY
  https://www.digitraffic.fi/  -  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Modified: converted from the passenger-train GTFS feed to a compact
  one-week timetable by theroadragetrip/railway_import.py (refreshed weekly). Provided as is,
  without warranty.

train_compositions.json.gz
  Source: Fintraffic / digitraffic.fi, license CC 4.0 BY
  https://www.digitraffic.fi/  -  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Modified: long-distance train compositions condensed to vehicle type,
  length and service flags by theroadragetrip/composition_import.py (refreshed weekly). Provided
  as is, without warranty.

sports_fixtures.json.gz
  Match schedules, results, teams, attendances and venue names retrieved
  2026-10-15 from the public Liiga and Veikkausliiga result services:
  https://www.liiga.fi/api/v2/schedule
  https://www.veikkausliiga.com/tilastot/
  Modified: the 2025-26 and 2026-27 Liiga seasons and the calendar-year
  2025 and 2026 Veikkausliiga seasons were normalized to UTC JSON with
  game crowd windows. Provided as is, without warranty. The source data
  remains copyright of Liiga, Veikkausliiga and their data providers.
  Status: UNRESOLVED - no licence or redistribution terms found; see
  LICENSE_AUDIT.md before a commercial release.

finnish_first_names.json.gz, finnish_surnames.json.gz
  Finnish first names and surnames from the population information system
  name data (Väestötietojärjestelmän suomalaisten nimiaineistot), Digital and
  Population Data Services Agency (DVV), https://nimipalvelu.dvv.fi/ and
  https://avoindata.suomi.fi/. License CC BY 4.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Modified: the most common names selected and converted to the game's JSON format.

kunnat.json.gz
  Finnish municipalities and urban areas (population, age structure, land
  area, coordinates) compiled from Wikipedia, © Wikipedia contributors,
  https://fi.wikipedia.org/. License CC BY-SA 4.0:
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
  Modified: tables combined (matched by municipality name, population
  density computed) and converted to the game's JSON format. This file is
  likewise licensed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Weather shown in the game (not stored here) is Finnish Meteorological
Institute (FMI) open data, CC BY 4.0, fetched at runtime when historical
weather is enabled.

godot/finland_minimap.svg (the city menu's map, shipped in the game)
  Drawn from OpenStreetMap motorway, trunk and primary roads by
  tools/osm/build_finland_minimap.py. © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL 1.0
  (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright).

sample_osm.json.gz (repository root, shipped with the server)
  Offline sample map for --use-sample. Its origin is not recorded:
  UNRESOLVED (see LICENSE_AUDIT.md).

Sounds and images: see src/theroadragetrip/sounds/LICENSE and LICENSE_AUDIT.md
for their status (several items are unresolved). The game shows these credits
under Credits in the main menu.
