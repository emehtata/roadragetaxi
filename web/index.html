01 / THE JOB

One city.
Infinite excuses.

The Road Rage Trip is a top-down taxi game powered by real-world OpenStreetMap data from Finland.

Pick up strangers, thread through living streets, dodge the chaos and get them there before your patience runs out. Every route is a little different. Every mistake costs.
