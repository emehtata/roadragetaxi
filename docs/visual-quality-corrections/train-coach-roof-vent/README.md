# Coach HVAC roof attachment (VQA-012)

Implementation and named gameplay verification are ready for independent review. None of the rejected gameplay attempts receives visual credit.

The call path is `entity_layer._trains_3d()` → `vehicle("train", ..., {"profile":"standard"})` → `place_model_car("train_coach")` → `merged_model()`. The source GLB already contains the gap: the black HVAC feet begin at **3.89 m**, above the painted roof at **3.57 m**. The cap is at 3.96–4.21 m and its fans rise to 4.234 m. Source nodes are joined by material; node names refer to unrelated original components, so the assembly was identified by connected geometry and inspected views.

`tools/repair_train_coach_vent.py` lowers just the existing HVAC box, feet, grilles and fans by **0.32 m**. The feet now meet the roof at 3.57 m, the cap retains its 25 cm thickness at 3.64–3.89 m and the fans remain exposed. There are no production renderer changes. All 914 modified vertices differ only in height. Every other binary byte (including other positions, normals, UVs, indices and embedded textures), and every material/node remain unchanged; only position accessor bounds are updated. Coach length, width, rail contact and geometry outside the HVAC assembly are preserved. Overall model height decreases from 4.234 to 3.914 m because the displaced assembly was its highest point.

The source/merged comparison confirms the gap survives merging and sampled LODs. [Initial regression](regression-before.log): **21 checks, 9 failures**. [Corrected regression](regression-after.log): **21 checks, 0 failures**, covering merged base plus the first three generated LODs, cap retention, four headings/elevation and pooled reuse. [Existing controls](regression-controls.log): **43 checks, 0 failures** for sizes, distance and material pooling; [train doors](train-doors.log) pass. [Targeted pytest](pytest.log): **6 passed**, including unchanged-data fingerprints, a reconstruction of the floating assembly followed by repair, exact original-file reproduction and idempotence (exact original reproduction was also asserted separately against the preserved original input). Both changed scripts parse; Linux and native Windows imports and `git diff --check` pass. Headless controls retain existing dummy-renderer/resource shutdown warnings.

## Named gameplay attempts

`vqa_train_coach_roof_vent_day` reuses the sourced Helsinki Eerikinkatu lineup pose (385325.8, 6671757.1, heading 0.59), whose earlier map query records 12 nearby lamps and 13 buildings. The coach is 18 m along that road and 0.9 m left. The existing top camera exposes its roof. Readiness identifies the pooled coach at that pose/heading, requires full-coach frustum/HUD/building clearance, useful coach and vent projected size, an elevated roof-view camera and no nearer dynamic body over the vent rectangle, in addition to normal map/date/time/weather readiness. It also requires an actively displayed 3D viewport, a visible mesh in that camera's cull mask, and coach rail contact above the local terrain. Disposable userdata is used and removed.

Four attempts are retained and excluded: the first was interrupted after its conservative whole-coach dynamic-body rectangle repeatedly overlapped other bodies; the check was then focused on the actual vent rectangle while full-coach HUD/building/frustum checks remained. The second reached measurement but a nearer body entered the vent rectangle, correctly failing readiness (279×204 px coach, 34.5×30.7 px vent). The third wrapper exited 143 during server startup for an undetermined external reason. The final retry crashed native Godot with `mem`/`mem_new` allocation failures in `sim_client.gd::_drain_lines`, followed by signal 11. No accepted screenshot exists. The heading guard was added after these failed attempts and parsed. A subsequent [recovery run](gameplay/recovery/vqa_train_coach_roof_vent_day/2026-06-20T1300_20261115-195959_3a3acf6b-dirty/result.json) reported `passed`, but independent screenshot inspection found no coach. It is **explicitly excluded**: the coach was buried beneath DEM terrain. The production `rail_height()` returns zero away from rail decks, while the street terrain is elevated; the old projected-bounds check failed to catch this. `route_3d_visible:false` and the recorded 2D camera position/zoom are normal for the existing top camera and do not identify a 2D renderer. The repaired readiness now rejects below-terrain coaches and records the actual active 3D camera/model origin/ground height.

The accepted scenario uses `server_args:["--no-auto-fetch","--no-terrain-dem80"]`, the existing flat-terrain option, to isolate this asset attachment. It retains `requires:{"buildings_in_view":1,"outdoor_3d":true}`, `zoom:1.6`, the existing top camera, clear weather/cloud0.1 at 2026-06-20T13:00 and a one-second measurement after 60 ready frames. [Accepted screenshot](gameplay/flat-terrain/vqa_train_coach_roof_vent_day/2026-06-20T1300_20261115-200438_3a3acf6b-dirty/screenshot.png) and [readiness](gameplay/flat-terrain/vqa_train_coach_roof_vent_day/2026-06-20T1300_20261115-200438_3a3acf6b-dirty/result.json) were inspected: **PASS**, empty errors, active3D=true, ground0m, model origin y0.028m, whole coach358×262px and vent44×39px at1280×720.
