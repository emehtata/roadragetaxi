# Catalog star sky

## Goal
Show real naked-eye stars through apparent visual magnitude 6.0 in dark areas, using a catalog-derived, ready-rendered celestial sky oriented for the player's place and game time.

## Files to change
- `tools/build_star_sky.py`: reproducible catalog filtering, epoch normalization, atlas generation and manifest checks.
- `godot/assets/sky/`: cleared compact coordinate data, generated atlas, source manifest, attribution and full licence text.
- `docs/star-sky.md`, `tools/license_inventory.json`: source selection, rights evidence, filter/count, astrometry, atlas settings and measured limits.
- `src/theroadragetrip/calendar.py`, `src/theroadragetrip/server/__init__.py`, `src/theroadragetrip/protocol.py`: cached celestial-to-map orientation at actual player coordinates and unambiguous UTC time; document state fields.
- `godot/main.gd`, `godot/map_layer.gd`, `godot/buildings_3d.gd`, `godot/sky_clouds.gdshader`: cached sky inputs, local light-pollution attenuation and star-atlas sampling behind clouds and existing bodies.
- `godot/i18n.gd`, `godot/startup.gd`: displayed source credits in Finnish, English and German.
- `tests/test_star_sky.py`, `godot/tests/star_sky.gd`, `godot/tests/manifest.txt`: meaningful source, orientation, visibility and resource-lifecycle checks.
- `godot/visual_test.gd`, `tools/visual_scenarios.json`, `docs/visual-testing.md`: named star scenarios with render-path readiness and recorded diagnostics.

## Behaviour / acceptance criteria
1. Select and pin an authoritative catalog with explicit redistribution rights compatible with the shipped game, source URLs/version/hash, schema, epoch/frame, photometry and proper-motion conventions. The research at `reports/Visual star catalog.md` is background: HYG v4.4's 5,071 raw `mag<=6` rows do not resolve its upstream Hipparcos rights, and Hipparcos I/239 is marked non-commercial. Do not bundle either without documented resolution. Investigate a cleared alternative such as Gaia, recording its bright-star incompleteness and valid G/BP/RP-to-V conversion; do not silently equate G with V. Unresolved rights or substantial bright-star gaps block release and must be reported with completed local work.
2. Produce the maximal eligible all-sky subset with finite coordinates and inclusive apparent Johnson V<=6.0 (or explicitly documented validated V estimates), stable source IDs, reference epoch, motion and color where available. Exclude Sol/solar-system/non-stellar records; define unresolved-double and missing/invalid-field policies. Record exact included/rejected counts and reasons; preserve otherwise eligible stars when color alone is missing. No random replacement stars or undocumented hand-picked omissions. A pinned manifest and rebuild reproduce byte-identical coordinate/atlas assets.
3. Bake a celestial-coordinate atlas offline, retaining all selected source directions and magnitude ordering; document projection, dimensions, point spread/filtering, color fallback, magnitude encoding and precision. Faint-star visibility must remain independently adjustable by magnitude: one global brightness multiplier is insufficient. Verify poles, RA seam, close pairs and magnitude-six dots survive sampling at supported gameplay resolutions without becoming large discs or vanishing through mipmaps. A batched alternative requires measured evidence that the atlas cannot meet these checks.
4. Derive the sky orientation from actual player latitude/longitude and authoritative UTC game time, handling Finnish DST/fold, sidereal rotation, catalog epoch/proper motion, date precession and EPSG:3067 grid convergence. Validate geometric directions above 10 degrees altitude within 0.1 degree of independently generated SOFA/ERFA reference fixtures for Polaris, Sirius, Vega and southern references at multiple dates/locations; no mirrored east/west sky, camera-locked stars or below-horizon stars. Document supported dates and omitted subpixel effects; a baked epoch must not silently remove proper motion.
5. A clear, moonless, fully dark rural site exposes eligible V<=6 stars above the horizon; twilight/daylight fade them smoothly and daytime hides them. Derive a bounded, cached local pollution estimate from existing working lamps/floodlights and surrounding mapped lighting/building context, so an unlit street within a city does not automatically count as pristine sky. State its heuristic limits without claiming measured Bortle classes. Brighter sites suppress faint stars before bright ones; transitions are smooth and monotonic, and a bright high Moon reduces visibility. Stars sit behind cloud density, disappear under full overcast, and never draw through terrain/buildings; existing Sun/Moon, precipitation and lighting behavior pass controls.
6. Follow `game-mechanics-optimizations/04_dirty_flags.md` and `41_2d_sprite_batching.md`: catalog parsing/rasterization occur offline, textures are shared/cached, and gameplay updates a compact transform/visibility input only when time/location/light inputs change at a documented cadence meeting criterion 4. No per-star nodes/lights, per-frame catalog scans, texture regeneration, network fetches or full-world light scans. Session restart, time jumps, camera changes and chunk streaming retain correct orientation and bounded resource counts.
7. Measure native Windows GPU before/after at identical pose/time/weather/resolution for dark rural and city views. Record p50/p95/p99/worst, draw calls, VRAM, texture size and sky/radiance-update behavior; investigate >10% p95 increase or new >16ms stalls before accepting atlas resolution/cadence. Account for existing moving cloud uniforms and radiance updates; WSL software rendering cannot establish the GPU budget.
8. Add real-map `stars_dark_rural_night`, `stars_city_night`, `stars_overcast_night` and `stars_day_control` scenarios, plus a same-place time-shift orientation check. Readiness requires the pinned atlas/source hash, correct observer basis and game date, pollution/magnitude threshold, actual sky camera, known catalog stars projected into unobscured sky pixels, and sampled rendered star/background contrast; loaded-row counts or uniforms alone cannot pass. Record expected star IDs/directions/projected positions, visibility and pixel contrast. Use moonless astronomical night for the rural case. Inspect passing native screenshots and retain all captures/research evidence locally.
9. Before merge, store full data licence text and transformation/attribution notices in asset docs and inventory, and display required attribution in all three shipped languages. Include separately licensed derived data/atlas obligations where applicable. Pass focused source/astrometry/renderer tests, existing cloud/Sun/Moon controls, inventory validation, scenario validation and diff checks; independent tester PASS precedes atomic commits/push. Preserve unrelated shared changes.

## Tests to add
- `tests/test_star_sky.py::test_pinned_catalog_filter_and_rights`: exact eligible counts/IDs, inclusive cutoff, Sol exclusion, rejected-field policy, hashes, source licence and deterministic rebuild; fails on unsafe/untraceable or arbitrary star data.
- `tests/test_star_sky.py::test_observer_basis_against_reference`: independent named-star alt/az fixtures across dates, DST/fold, hemispheres, player travel and grid convergence; fails on stale epoch/location, time-zone or axis errors.
- `godot/tests/star_sky.gd::test_atlas_projection_and_magnitude_visibility`: seam/poles, source-pixel directions, magnitude ordering, dark/city/twilight/Moon/cloud cutoffs and shader integration; fails on random/shifted stars or whole-atlas-only dimming.
- `godot/tests/star_sky.gd::test_cached_sky_lifecycle`: bounded shared resources and changed-input updates through time jumps, session reload and camera/chunk transitions; fails on per-star nodes, repeated rasterization or stale sky state.
- `tests/test_star_sky.py::test_star_scenarios_require_rendered_catalog_stars`: named real-map poses, known-star projected contrast and day/overcast/city controls; fails if an empty or wrong-direction sky can pass readiness.

## Out of scope
Planets, constellations/labels, Milky Way/nebulae, meteors, aurora, new light-pollution datasets, exposure-system redesign, astrophotographic effects, traffic behavior and historical visual-audit changes. Stars are catalog data rendered as a sky asset; no unrelated procedural-object replacement work.
