# Refueling

Stop within eight metres of a fuel pump, switch off the engine (E), get out
(F), and press G beside the taxi. Fuel flows at 30 litres per minute of
simulation elapsed time (0.5 L/s), independently of the accelerated calendar.
The notification reports the delivered litres and cost while filling.

Press G again to stop filling. Filling also stops when the tank is full, the balance runs out, the driver gets
back in or moves more than eight metres from the taxi, the engine starts,
or the taxi leaves pump range. Only delivered fuel is charged, with rounding
applied to the whole fill rather than separately to every tick.

The dashboard fuel needle rests at E with the engine off and displays the
actual tank level once the engine is running.
